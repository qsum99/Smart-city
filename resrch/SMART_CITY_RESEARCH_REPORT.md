# PART II — QUANTUM COMPUTING FOR SMART CITIES

## 1. Executive Summary

Quantum computing for cities is a growing but mostly **proof-of-concept** field with six pillars: (1) traffic control & routing, (2) smart grid optimization, (3) QML prediction, (4) QKD security, (5) 5G/6G resource allocation, (6) urban planning/sensor placement. Nearly all work uses **hybrid quantum-classical** designs (QAOA, VQE, annealing + classical solvers) due to **NISQ-era** limits (50–1,000+ qubits, no full error correction). Real deployments: Volkswagen/D-Wave traffic pilots (Beijing 2018, Lisbon 2019), China's metropolitan QKD networks. Honest benchmarks (de Valk 2026) show quantum advantage at city scale **not yet demonstrated**.

## 2. Quantum Fundamentals for Cities

| Paradigm | Role |
|---|---|
| Gate-based (superconducting/ trapped-ion) | QAOA/VQE circuits; IBM Qiskit, IonQ, Quantinuum |
| Quantum annealing (D-Wave) | Native QUBO/Ising solver for combinatorial city problems |
| QAOA | Leading hybrid algorithm for QUBO urban problems (depth p=1–5) |
| VQE | Variational hybrid eigenvalue/optimization loops |
| QML / QNN | QLSTM, QSVM, VQC, QCNN for time series |
| Quantum RL (QRL) | QAOA policy networks, quantum DRL navigation |
| QKD (BB84/E91, QSDC) | Info-theoretic security for city IoT/grid |
| NISQ (Preskill 2018) | Core constraint: noise, shallow circuits |
| QEC / qLDPC (IBM) | ~90% overhead reduction; fault tolerance ~2029 |
| Cloud quantum | IBM Quantum, Amazon Braket, D-Wave Leap |

**Workflow:** City problem → LP/QUBO/Ising → QPU/hybrid solver → classical post-processing → compare vs Gurobi/annealing/heuristics.

## 9. Challenges

2. **Reward shaping** — theory-grounded (PressLight, G2P).
3. **Non-stationarity** — CTDE, parameter sharing, action cascading.
4. **Safety** — CMDP/Lagrangian, minimum-green compliance.
5. **Scalability** — MPLight (2,510 lights), GNN inductive bias, hierarchical decomposition.
6. **Sample efficiency** — model-based RL, meta-RL (MetaLight), imitation (DemoLight), offline RL (OffLight).

## Top 10 Cross-Cutting Research Opportunities

1. **Quantum + RL (QRL)** for traffic signal control — almost unexplored; combine QAOA policy networks with PressLight-style rewards.
2. **LLM+RL city agents** — Traffic-R1 shows deployment viability; extend to energy, water, transit.
3. **Quantum × digital twin × urban energy/mobility** — frameworks proposed (Lemo 2025, Alreshidi 2025) but few validated on real data.
4. **Honest quantum advantage benchmarking** at city scale (de Valk 2026 line of work; QOBLIB extension).
5. **FL + TinyML** for privacy-preserving city sensing at the edge (non-IID, communication cost open).
6. **Cross-domain foundation models** linking mobility–energy–environment.
7. **Safe/offline RL** for live-city deployment without risky online exploration.
8. **QKD + PQC hybrid** security architecture for municipal IoT procurement.
9. **Crime/public-safety QML** — an identified gap (no dedicated papers found).
10. **Social-science/governance literature** — only 7% of quantum-city output; equity, procurement, institutional readiness.
